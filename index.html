About Kattan Chaya

Kattan Chaya കട്ടൻ ചായ

Kattan chaya — literally “black tea” in Malayalam — is the soul of Kerala. Brewed strong and dark, without milk or sugar, it is served at roadside chaaya kadas across Keralam. This is your little tea stall on the internet.

Kerala nostalgia കേരളം

For Malayalis everywhere, this is your digital chaaya kada. Mix monsoon rain, thunder, crackling fire, chirping birds, wind and old conversations — the sounds of a Keralam evening.

Old Malayalam songs പഴയ പാട്ടുകൾ

Listen to beloved songs from the golden era while the sounds of Kerala play in the background. Perfect for studying, working, or sitting with a cup of kattan chai.

Free Malayalam ASMR സൗജന്യം

No login, no interruptions. Just the sounds of Kerala — kattan chaya, chaaya kada, rain, fire and Malayalam songs. Made with chai love by Alok.
