From our little corner

More than a cup of tea

The chaaya kada ചായക്കട

In Kerala, the tea shop is a place to pause. Someone knows your order. A radio hums in the corner. The rain can wait. We made this little digital version for Malayalis far from home — and anyone who needs a quieter moment.

Sounds like home നാടിന്റെ ശബ്ദം

Let the monsoon roll through, add a crackle from the stove, a few crickets beyond the awning. The sound mixer is yours to shape: soft enough to study, slow enough to sleep, familiar enough to feel at home.

Take it slow പതുക്കെ

Use a sleep timer for the end of a long day. Leave the rain on while you read, work, or watch the last bus roll past. There's no right way to sit here. Just stay as long as you like.

Made with chai love സ്നേഹത്തോടെ

Kattan Chaya is a small independent project by Alok. It's free to visit, needs no login, and is built for anyone who misses the warm light of a Kerala evening.
